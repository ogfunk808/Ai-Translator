📖 How to Use OG TRANSLATOR 3D

Welcome to the official user guide & tutorial! Learn how to navigate and utilize every feature of OG TRANSLATOR 3D efficiently.

1
🌐

Select Languages & Swap

Click on the Source or Target language selectors to open the searchable 100+ language menu. Use the central Swap (⇄) button to instantly switch source and target languages.

2
✍️

Type or Paste Text

Type your text in the left input box for real-time instant translation. You can also click the 📋 Paste button to import text directly from your clipboard.

3
🎙️

Use 3D Audio Voice AI

Click the microphone button (🎙️ Speak) to activate speech recognition. Allow browser mic access, and watch the 3D WebGL particle mesh expand and pulse to your voice in real-time!

4
🔊

Listen to Audio Pronunciation

Click the 🔊 Listen Audio button on either source or target card to hear native neural voice synthesis. Adjust voice pitch and speed in the Settings menu (⚙️).

5
📖

Dictionary & Alternatives

Click the 📖 Book icon inside the output box to open the Dictionary & Synonyms Drawer for alternative word choices, definitions, and context phrasing.

6
🪐

Control 3D WebGL Globe

Use the 3D Scene Controls bar below the hero section to toggle Auto Rotation (ON/OFF), cycle Particle Flow density, or Reset 3D Perspective.

⚙️ Advanced Features & Settings Guide

1. Phonetic & Romanized Script Output

When translating into languages with non-Latin scripts (such as Japanese, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Korean, or Russian), OG TRANSLATOR 3D automatically displays Romanized phonetic pronunciation below the main translated text box.

2. History & Favorites Management

Never lose a translation query! Your translations are recorded automatically in your local session history.

  • Click ⭐ Star to save any translation query to your Favorites.
  • Click 📜 History in the top header to view past queries or clear your history log.

3. Customizing Voice Pitch & Speech Speed

Navigate to Settings (⚙️) in the top navigation bar to adjust:

  • Speech Output Pitch: Set from 0.5 (deep tone) to 1.5 (higher tone).
  • Speech Output Speed: Set from 0.5x (slower pronunciation) to 1.5x (faster speech).
  • Translation Provider Engine: Switch between OG Free Neural Mesh, custom Gemini 1.5 Flash API, or custom OpenAI GPT-4o API.
