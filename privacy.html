🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026. Your privacy and data confidentiality are of paramount importance to us.

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

At OG TRANSLATOR 3D, accessible from https://ogtranslator.com, user privacy is one of our foundational priorities. This Privacy Policy document describes the types of information that are collected, recorded, and utilized by OG TRANSLATOR 3D in compliance with international data protection frameworks, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information Collection & Transient Processing

OG TRANSLATOR 3D operates with a privacy-by-design framework. We do not store, archive, or monetize your personal text inputs, translation queries, or voice recordings on our servers.

  • Text Translation Data: Source text and target translations are processed transiently in your local browser session.
  • Voice Recognition Audio: Speech audio captured via the Web Speech API is converted to text directly within your browser interface and is not retained after rendering.
  • Local Storage: User preferences (such as custom speech pitch, speed, selected translation engine, and favorited phrases) are saved strictly inside your device's browser localStorage.

3. Google AdSense, AdMob & Advertising Cookies

OG TRANSLATOR 3D uses Google AdSense and Google AdMob (Publisher Account ID: ca-pub-5350198706036162) to serve contextually relevant advertisements.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our platform based on a user's visit to this and other websites on the Internet:

  • DART Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on browsing history.
  • Opting Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com.
  • For more information on how Google manages advertising data, visit Google's Privacy & Terms policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third-Party Web Analytics & Partners

We work with trusted third-party analytics and ad network providers (such as Google Analytics and Monetag) to measure website performance, WebGL rendering throughput, and user engagement. These services may log standard non-personally identifiable technical information, such as browser type, device category, language preferences, IP address, and timestamp access logs.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request deletion of personal data.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request restricted data handling.

6. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose categories of data collected and request that personal data not be sold. OG TRANSLATOR 3D does NOT sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Protecting children online is essential. OG TRANSLATOR 3D does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal information on our platform, please contact us immediately so we can promptly purge such information.
