❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is OG TRANSLATOR 3D completely free?

Yes! OG TRANSLATOR 3D is 100% free to use for unlimited translations across 100+ languages without mandatory subscription or registration.

2. How does the 3D WebGL Audio-Reactive globe work?

Our application uses Three.js to render a holographic planetary mesh and particle field. When speech recognition is active, real-time microphone volume frequencies modulate the 3D particle positions dynamically.

3. Which browsers support voice speech input & audio output?

Voice recognition and Speech Synthesis (TTS) are supported out of the box in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Safari, and Opera browsers.

4. How do I adjust speech audio pitch and playback speed?

Click the Settings (⚙️) button in the top navigation header to customize speech output pitch and speed between 0.5x and 1.5x.
